How to play

You're a pirate kid with a Hat Launcher. Bonk the Backrooms bots with spinning hats!

Got bonked? Don't worry! You turn into a ghost 👻 and get 60 seconds to build a fort right where you fell (look for the glowing pirate flag).

When time's up — or you press RESPAWN NOW — you pop back in inside your fort with full hearts and a shield. That's Bonk-to-Build!

Most bonks in 5 minutes wins.
