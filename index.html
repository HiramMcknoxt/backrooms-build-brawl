How to play

Capture the Flag: Steal the other team's flag and bring it to your pedestal while your flag is home. First to 3 captures wins.

Got bonked? Don't worry! You turn into a ghost 👻 and get 60 seconds to build a fort where you fell (look for the glowing pirate flag). In Capture the Flag you can also build at your own flag.

Press RESPAWN NOW when you're ready and pop back in inside your fort with a shield. That's Bonk-to-Build!

Bonk Brawl: Most bonks in five minutes wins. A tie goes to Sudden Bonk.

Find your way: the red EXIT sign is the middle of the Yellow Rooms. Captain Clark's ship is in the Water Rooms. Check the map in the corner.

Controls

WASD move · Mouse look · Click throw hat / place block

Space jump · Shift sprint · B build mode · 1–6 or wheel swaps weapons in fight mode. 1–5 selects blocks in build mode.

Right-click / R / X remove block · Enter respawn now (ghost)

No mouse lock: QE/←→ turn, ↑↓ look, F throw / place

Ghost: Space float up · C float down · Z/V choose anchor in CTF

P/Esc pause · M mute · ` debug

Controller: L-stick move · R-stick look · RT throw/place · LT remove · A jump · Y build · LB/RB block · D-pad ←/→ weapon or ghost anchor · X respawn now · Start pause

Bonk Voyage: E / right click / pad B / touch Action to board or use stations. Hold it to repair or open the chest. Helm: movement controls. Cannon: aim and fire as usual, 1–3 or D-pad / Ammo to switch. Use arrow keys for keyboard-only look.

Phone: Use the left stick to move and drag the right side to look. Tap Weapon or Block to cycle. Hold Fire or Place to repeat. Landscape gives you more room.
